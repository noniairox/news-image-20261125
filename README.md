A newspaper front page summing up Wednesday 25 November 2026 in one image.
Lead story: oil tankers hit naval mines and exploded in the Strait of Hormuz.
Also covered: US tariff proposals on 14 partners, the Wall Street slide,
China flying its first foreign astronaut, the killing of a Hamas security
chief in Gaza, Huawei's conviction in a US trial, and the COP31 talks
failing to reach a deal.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the shipped
`entry.png` to the output directory. It runs with no network and writes
byte-identical output on every run.
